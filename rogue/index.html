                                                                                                Solo Rotation

                                                                                                1. Stealth, get behind the mob. Premeditation (22+) for 2 free Combo Points.
                                                                                                2. Open with Ambush (dagger in main hand).
                                                                                                3. Build Combo Points with Sinister Strike.
                                                                                                4. Finish a weakened mob with Eviscerate. High-health mob: Slice and Dice instead.
                                                                                                5. Move to the next mob while Remorseless Attacks is still up (+40% crit on the next Ambush).
                                                                                                6. Need a fast kill: Gouge, Vanish, another Ambush. Interrupt with Kick.
                                                                                                7. Bad pull: Vanish to escape. Dangerous mob: Cheap Shot, then Kidney Shot (30+).

                                                                                                Staying Alive Solo

                                                                                                • Evasion on a dangerous mob; Sprint and Vanish to get away.
                                                                                                • Sap no longer breaks Stealth in Forever: Sap one mob out of a pull, then Ambush another.
                                                                                                • Energy, not mana: no water needed, but you eat a lot. Stay Well Fed for the extra XP.
                                                                                                • First Aid bandages and potions; Rogues can't remove bleeds or diseases otherwise.

                                                                                                Mining + Engineering ✔

                                                                                                Mining feeds Engineering. When you mine a vein, keep both the ore and the stone: smelt ore into bars for parts, grind stone into blasting powder for explosives. Some recipes still need cloth or leather.

                                                                                                Mining perk: +5% total health ("Made of Metal"). Two fan sources agree; Blizzard hasn't published it.

                                                                                                Engineering Ranks

                                                                                                • Apprentice: up to 75.
                                                                                                • Journeyman: up to 150. Needs skill 50 and level 10.
                                                                                                • Expert: up to 225. Needs skill 125 and level 20 (Springspindle Fizzlegear, Tinker Town, Ironforge).
                                                                                                • Artisan: up to 300. Needs skill 200 and level 35 (Gadgetzan or Mount Hyjal).

                                                                                                Train the next rank as soon as you qualify, before a big crafting batch.

                                                                                                Solo Picks for War Po

                                                                                                • Bombs and grenades: damage plus a short stun around the target (Iron Grenade stuns up to 3 sec; more damage breaks it). All bombs share one 1-minute cooldown.
                                                                                                • Target Dummy: drop it when a pull goes bad; it taunts nearby enemies off you while you get away.
                                                                                                • Bomb satchels: thrown weapons for the ranged slot that explode on hit. Satchel of Copper Bombs needs skill 85 and level 13.
                                                                                                • Camp items: Reagent Bot at skill 20 (camp reagent vendor). Repair Bot at 140 (repairs too); its Blueprint drops from Sneed in The Deadmines.

                                                                                                Icy Veins (Iron Grenade, Target Dummy, camp items), Mobalytics (shared cooldown, satchels, Repair Bot blueprint)

Gnomish leans to utility gadgets (net, shrink ray, battle chicken). Goblin leans to explosives (Sapper Charge, mortar, rocket helmet). Your call when you get there.
