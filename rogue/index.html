  • Start in Thendal Village, Zephras Isle (every Skyborne starts here; needs the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher). Rogue trainer Akeri Duskblade, next to the village; trainers here only teach up to level 6. Sinister Strike builds Combo Points, Eviscerate spends them. [wowforever.be, Wowhead]
  • Walk on Air: jump off anything high and glide for 10 sec. It saves a lot of walking on the island. [wowforever.be]
  • Agitators (Yala Windwatcher, north valley): the Elemental Convergence for your Horde quest is right there. Cast Skysight on it for the Charged buff. [wowforever.be]
  • Ruleset: Roleplaying (your realm, CAMWOW US2 Realm 11 RP). New spells don't jump onto your bars, so drag each one from your spellbook (P). Training 1-60 costs about 206-207g total. [Blizzard dev notes, Nov 2]
  • Stealth turns your main bar to a separate stealth bar that starts empty, so your buttons seem to vanish. While stealthed, drag Sinister Strike to 1 and Stealth to 2. The Bars tab has both layouts. [FlareUI changelog, Nov 12]

                                                                                    Horde 101

                                                                                    First time on the Horde. Short version: you land next to Thunder Bluff, Orgrimmar is the main hub, Undercity is across the sea, and zeppelins connect them.

                                                                                    Skyborne (Windshaper) Racials

                                                                                    • Walk on Air: glide down for 10 sec, steering as you go. Great for a loner taking shortcuts off cliffs.
                                                                                    • Skysight: a short run-speed buff. Cast it at an elemental convergence and you're Charged for an hour, which makes every Skysight stronger. Charged lasts through death.
                                                                                    • Wind Blessed: passive melee, ranged and spell haste.
                                                                                    • Elemental Insight: 5% more damage to Elementals.

                                                                                    The numbers changed during beta:

                                                                                    • Blizzard's Skyborne article (Sept): Wind Blessed 1% haste, Skysight +10% run speed.
                                                                                    • Blizzard beta notes (Oct 26): Wind Blessed 2%; Skysight +15% for 15 sec, 30% while Charged (1 hour).
                                                                                    • ForeverDB's read of the live client (build 1.60.1.70938, Nov 13): Wind Blessed 2%, Skysight +15% for 30 sec, doubled while Charged.

                                                                                    So: 2% haste and 15% speed are the current values; the exact Skysight duration differs between sources. Walk on Air's cooldown is listed as 90 sec by fan sites and 2 min by Warcraft Wiki. Check your tooltips.

                                                                                    Getting Off the Island

                                                                                    1. Finish the island story at about 12 and hand in with Ayessa Dawnsinger.
                                                                                    2. Take the skyship from Valanaar's western dock (the eastern one goes to Dalaran, meant for the Alliance). Ships come about every 5 minutes.
                                                                                    3. You land at Skywatcher Plateau, northwest Mulgore, just north of Thunder Bluff. You can ride the same ship back to the island any time.
                                                                                    4. Walk south into Thunder Bluff, take the elevator up, and talk to Tal, the wind rider master, to fly to Orgrimmar (about 50 copper).

                                                                                    The island gives a quest chain, Exploring the Horde, starting with Nazgrel in Orgrimmar's Grommash Hold. It walks you to each Horde leader, so you get to see all three cities.

                                                                                    The Three Cities

                                                                                    • Thunder Bluff (Mulgore, Kalimdor): the tauren city on tall mesas joined by rope bridges and elevators. Closest to where you land. Horde Skyborne start Friendly here. Wind rider master Tal. Mace and gun trainer Ansekhwa, Mining trainer Brek Stonehoof.
                                                                                    • Orgrimmar (Durotar, Kalimdor): the orc capital and the main Horde hub. Your rogue trainer is Shenthul in the Cleft of Shadow, and he starts the poison quests at 20. Engineering trainers for every rank. Zeppelins leave from here.
                                                                                    • Undercity (Tirisfal Glades, Eastern Kingdoms): the Forsaken city under the ruins of Lordaeron. You get there by zeppelin from Orgrimmar. Sword trainer Archibald, Engineering trainers, and the flight master for the Eastern Kingdoms side.

                                                                                    You start Neutral with Orgrimmar, Undercity and the Darkspear Trolls, so they'll warm up as you quest. ForeverDB also lists Horde rogue trainers in Thunder Bluff and Undercity.

                                                                                    Action Bars: Normal vs Stealth

                                                                                    Why your buttons disappear: when you Stealth, the game flips Action Bar 1 to a separate stealth bar. It's a different set of 10 buttons on the same keys, and it starts empty. Your normal buttons aren't gone; they come back the moment Stealth ends.

                                                                                    Fix (once): press Stealth, open your spellbook (P), and drag spells onto the empty bar. They stay there for good. Right now at level 2: drag Sinister Strike to 1 and Stealth to 2.

                                                                                    Same key, same job on both bars: 1 opens or builds, 2 is Stealth, 9 is Sprint, 0 is Vanish. In Forever, Sap, Pick Pocket and Distract don't break Stealth.

                                                                                    The swap is automatic: FlareUI changelog (Nov 12: "The game now turns Action Bar 1 to its stealth page by itself"). It was broken in one beta build (forever-bugs #356, Oct 16); your bar does swap, so it works on your client. Stealth rules: Conquest Capped. I couldn't find a Blizzard Options setting in Forever that turns the swap off, so none is listed here.

                                                                                    Combo Points

                                                                                    Built in: in Forever, combo points live on the mob you built them on. Switch targets and you start from zero on the new one. The default UI shows them as 5 small dots on the target frame, next to the mob's portrait, and they light up as you build. Forever's settings also have a Personal Resource Display section, where a rogue can keep combo points showing near the character.

                                                                                    If the dots are missing: type /run SetCVar("comboPointLocation", 1), then /reload. That's the setting the addon below turns on for target-frame combo points.

                                                                                    Addon pick: Combo Points on Plate (CurseForge, Forever, v1.9.0). It puts the dots on the mob's nameplate, right where you're looking, and keeps the target-frame dots too. Choose the Classic or Blizzard style for a vanilla look. Type /cpp for options.
